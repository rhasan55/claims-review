Before a correction reaches a biller

Hold a correction when its evidence does not match.

A plausible sentence is not sufficient. The policy must apply to this payer, plan and service date, and the proposed value must exist in the supplied evidence.

What I would measure in the first sprint
  1. Pick one payer and denial family with the operations team.
  2. Reconstruct what was known at the original decision. Audit remittance reversals, repeated 835 events and payment allocation.
  3. Build a blinded, biller-reviewed set for policy applicability and supported actions.
  4. Run in shadow mode. Track review time, follow-rate, abstention and matured net recovery.
What this demonstration does not establish
No claim of real recovery lift, production security, HIPAA compliance or LLM reasoning accuracy. The queue model is observational. Policies are three small fixtures, not a real retrieval corpus.
In real data, unworked claims may have no outcome. Selection bias and action-specific effects need a prospective test before using this score as a revenue forecast.
