Which claim gets
worked next?

A biller has time for 100 denied claims. The largest balance is one place to start. A calibrated recovery estimate gives another.

This example follows the decision from a claim snapshot to a work queue, then checks whether a proposed correction has evidence behind it.

Synthetic data throughout. No Red Sky Health data, real payer rules or patient records. Model predictions are computed in Python. The response examples are fixed test cases, not live LLM output. Nothing is submitted to a payer.

Before a correction reaches a biller

Make the evidence inspectable.

A plausible sentence is not sufficient. The policy must apply to this payer, plan and service date, and the proposed value must exist in the supplied evidence.

Scope the policyPayer, plan, effective date and availability at decision.
Inspect the candidateStructured action, exact quote, evidence IDs and proposed change.
Check the contractReject unsupported values and unauthorized actions.
Route for reviewSupported proposals still require a person. No automatic filing.

Candidate output

A fixture supplied to the Python validator. Identifiers and policies are fictional.

Available claim evidence
Rationale is untrusted text. The validator checks the actionable fields, not whether free-form reasoning is medically or semantically correct.


What I would measure in the first sprint
  1. Pick one payer and denial family with the operations team.
  2. Reconstruct what was known at the original decision. Audit remittance reversals, repeated 835 events and payment allocation.
  3. Build a blinded, biller-reviewed set for policy applicability and supported actions.
  4. Run in shadow mode. Track review time, follow-rate, abstention and matured net recovery.
What this demonstration does not establish
No claim of real recovery lift, production security, HIPAA compliance or LLM reasoning accuracy. The queue model is observational. Policies are three small fixtures, not a real retrieval corpus.
In real data, unworked claims may have no outcome. Selection bias and action-specific effects need a prospective test before using this score as a revenue forecast.
